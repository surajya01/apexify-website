AquaTrack

AquaTrack is a complete swimming management ecosystem built entirely in-house — comprising a Flutter Mobile App for athletes and a Flutter Admin Panel for club admins, officials, and federation managers. Both platforms share a unified Firebase backend and cover the full lifecycle of competitive swimming: athlete registration, event & meet management, heat scheduling, results recording, FINA point calculations, rankings, performance tracking, bulk data imports, and membership management.

engineering My Role — Full Product Development

I designed and developed the complete AquaTrack ecosystem — both the Flutter Mobile App and the Flutter Admin Panel — end-to-end. My work spanned the full product: mobile UI/UX, admin dashboard, all data management screens, real-time Firestore integrations, Firebase Authentication, bulk Excel & LENEX import pipeline, athlete management workflows, event & registration systems, results & race history, schedule & session management, rankings & FINA point calculations, membership management, and the shared Firebase backend architecture connecting both platforms.

Full athlete profiles, registration workflows, club assignments, and personal bests tracking.
