Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Apexify handles personal information when you visit our website, scope architecture, or contact us about a software engineering engagement.

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Important Notice: This is a general website privacy notice. Review and adapt it to reflect your actual data practices, third-party cloud vendors, and regulatory obligations before deploying customer-facing environments.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, work email address, phone number, company name, and project requirements when you submit an inquiry through our contact form. We may also collect technical analytics (browser type, device telemetry, pages viewed) strictly necessary to maintain website availability, performance, and security.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information exclusively to review technical requirements, respond to inquiries, prepare custom engineering proposals, maintain website security, and communicate regarding scheduled sprints. We never sell, lease, or monetize client data under any circumstances.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal data. Information is only shared with trusted cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., hosting, encrypted email routing) bound by rigorous confidentiality agreements who require access to deliver their services. Disclosures may also occur when mandated by applicable law or valid legal process.

4. Data Retention & Zero-Trust Security

We retain inquiry data only as long as necessary to facilitate technical review and fulfill contractual obligations. All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 standards. Our team enforces strict zero-trust access controls across all internal repositories and communication channels.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You retain the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of any personal information provided to Apexify. You may also opt out of promotional communications at any point by contacting our technical team.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to external client case studies or technical documentation. External services operate under their own privacy protocols, which we advise reviewing independently.

7. Policy Amendments

We may revise this privacy statement periodically to reflect changing regulatory standards. Any modifications will be documented on this page alongside a revised effective date.

8. Contact Information

If you have inquiries regarding this privacy policy or wish to exercise data rights, reach out to our team at apexifyinfos@gmail.com or submit a message via our contact page.
